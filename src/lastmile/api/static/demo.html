03 · Causal Targeting: The 4 Uplift Quadrants

Technical Innovation 30%

A standard risk score measures $P(\text{default})$. A causal uplift model estimates $\tau = P(\text{cure} \mid \text{action}) - P(\text{cure} \mid \text{no action})$. Only accounts in Quadrant 1 justify spending scarce team capacity.

Holdout Qini > 0 (Beats Random Targeting)

🟢 1. Persuadables (High Positive Uplift)

–
Will default if left alone, but cure when contacted. High incremental dollar recovery per agent-minute.
Action: Prioritize for high-touch voice call, tailored payment plan, or SMS outreach.

⚪ 2. Sure Things (High Baseline Cure)

–
Will cure on their own without intervention. High risk grade may trigger false alarms, but contact adds 0 incremental dollars.
Action: Do Not Contact (Save staff minutes; zero wasted cost).

🔴 3. Lost Causes (Severe Inelasticity)

–
Will default regardless of outreach. High balance makes greedy systems call them first, wasting hundreds of agent minutes for zero cure lift.
Action: Suppress standard collections; route directly to legal or specialized recovery.

🟡 4. Sleeping Dogs (Negative Uplift)

–
Outreach triggers reactance or anger, accelerating charge-off or dispute. Contact actually harms portfolio recovery.
Action: Strictly suppressed by the optimizer (0 negative value actions assigned).

06 · Clean Division of Labor: AI Orchestrates, Models & Solvers Do the Math

Responsible AI

🤖 What the AI (LLM & Agents) Does

  • Workflow Orchestration: Coordinates the 8 stages, checks preconditions, and manages handoffs.
  • Outreach Script Drafting: Drafts empathetic, compliance-aligned rationale & script templates per segment × action.
  • Plain-Language Translation: Explains solver trade-offs to managers and compliance reviewers without technical jargon.
  • Strict Constraint: The LLM is NEVER sent raw account numbers and is forbidden from generating unverified digits.

🔢 What the Math Models & Solvers Do

  • Causal Estimation: Bagged T-Learner (Gradient Boosted) calculates uplift $\tau$ and 90% confidence intervals.
  • Assignment Optimization: CBC Mixed-Integer Linear Solver maximizes total portfolio recovery subject to capacity.
  • Risk Simulation: Monte Carlo runs 2,000 iterations for P10–P90 distributions; SimPy models floor queue dynamics.
  • Zero Hallucinations: 100% of figures (balances, DPD, uplift, minutes) are stamped with cryptographic provenance.
