Collections decisions

Every morning, a collections team has to decide who to call.

Last Mile works out who to contact, how, and why — then a person approves every action before anything is sent.

Chapter 1

The problem

The bank already knows who is likely to fall behind. What it doesn't know is what to do about it.

–

members behind on a payment today

Each one could get a text, a call, a payment plan, a hardship referral — or nothing at all.

agent minutes available

A small team can only reach a fraction of those members in a day.

?

who, how, and why

Calling the riskiest accounts first feels right, but some would pay anyway, some never will, and some react badly to being chased.

Not every member responds the same way to contact

Under the hood. A risk score estimates the chance an account defaults. Last Mile adds a second estimate — uplift: how much a specific action changes the chance the member catches up. The value of an action is uplift × expected loss if the account does not cure − cost.
Chapter 2

Meet the team

Eight AI agents each own one job. None of them can do another agent's work — and none of them has the final say.

Agents coordinate and explain

They run the steps in order, check the work, and write the reasons in plain language.

Models and a planner do the maths

Every number comes from a statistical model, a simulation or an optimisation solver — never from the AI.

A person decides

The collections manager approves, edits or rejects every action. Nothing reaches a member without it.
